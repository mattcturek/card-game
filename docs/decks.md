# Event Decks

Last updated: 2026-11-26 (America/Chicago)

Status: Structure locked. **Tier 1 stories drafting** with Matt.

## Structure (locked)

Tile types **0 / 1 / 2 / 3**. Landing on **0** → no Event. **1 / 2 / 3** → draw from that Tier Event deck.

| Deck | Drawn on tile | Difficulty |
|------|---------------|------------|
| **Tier 1 Event** | **1** | Easy / mild |
| **Tier 2 Event** | **2** | Harder / more hurtful |
| **Tier 3 Event** | **3** | Hardest to overcome |

### 1. Friendly Hail
A battered freighter drops out of the lane and flashes a friendly transponder. Their engineer offers a spare fuel cell “for the road,” no questions asked—just the kind of frontier kindness that keeps small ships flying.


### 2. Charted Eddy
Nav charts mark a gentle gravity eddy along this route. Ride it and the hull hums; you steal a little distance without burning hard.

### 3. Market Rumor
On an open channel, a dockside chatterbox brags about ships they’ve seen. You catch a scrap of gossip about another player’s heading—nothing mission-critical, but enough to watch their vector.

### 4. Minor Hull Scratch
Micrometeor grit kisses the hull. Alarms chirp, then quiet. Paint’s ruined; systems are fine. A reminder that space always takes a small tax.

### 5. Calibration Drift
Sensors wander a few degrees off true. For a moment the stars look wrong, then the AI settles. You’ll trust the next reading a little less.

### 6. Courier Packet
A relay buoy pings you with a low-priority mail run—medical forms, colony mail, nothing glamorous. Completing it earns you a small favor someone will remember.

### 7. Quiet Watch
The night cycle passes without a contact. Stars, static, coffee. Sometimes the sector lets you breathe.

### 8. Dockmaster’s Tip
A half-smile and a half-credit buy a quiet word from a dockmaster who “doesn’t get involved.” They glance at the board and tell you what number sits on one adjacent tile.

### 9. Spare Parts Bin
You drift past a junked pod and pull a cheap regulator that still works. Keep it for the next mild snag—Tier 1 trouble you’ll shrug off once.

### 10. First Contact Ping
An automated probe asks for registry and intent. Answer straight and it logs you clean. Bluff, and you might draw hotter attention next time you land something worse.


## Tier 2 Event (10) — harder

1. **Customs Sweep** — Frigates demand a scan; dump a cargo token, pay a bribe, or take a delay (skip your next move).
2. **Micro-Meteor Swarm** — Shields take a hit; discard one held Event or lose your next interrupt.
3. **False Beacon** — A spoofed distress pulls you one hex off-course toward a hazard of the active player’s choice.
4. **Crew Fatigue** — Double shifts catch up; next mission check at a destination is harder.
5. **Syndicate Tax** — Local toughs “offer protection”; pay resources or they mark your ship (others may interrupt you freely once).
6. **Nebula Static** — Comms black out; you cannot play interrupts until after your next turn.
7. **Rival Shadow** — Another player may immediately move one hex closer to you or your revealed path.
8. **Leaking Coolant** — Ongoing: each of your turns, roll; on a failure, lose one movement point from your die.
9. **Contraband Sniff** — If you’re carrying mission cargo (e.g. Cold Cargo), reveal whether you’re “loaded” without naming the mission.
10. **Dock Lockout** — Next time you land on a station/outpost tile, you must spend an extra turn or a card to undock.

## Tier 3 Event (10) — hardest

1. **Raider Ambush** — Fight or flee: win a hard check or be sent back halfway to center and discard two cards.
2. **Core Breach Scare** — Emergency shutdown; skip your next full turn and discard all Tier 1 Events in hand.
3. **Identity Burn** — Your registry is flagged sector-wide; every landing on 2+ forces an extra Tier 1 draw against you.
4. **Warp Shear** — Violent turbulence; place your ship on a random adjacent hex and draw another Event of that tile’s number.
5. **Betrayal Signal** — Someone leaks a clue about your secret mission to all players (one keyword from your card).
6. **Boarders Away** — Hostile boarding attempt; fail a hard check and an opponent chooses one: steal a card, force a reveal of your tile target type, or pin you in place next turn.
7. **Black-Site Interdict** — Corporate or navy blockade: you cannot enter mission destination hexes until you resolve a costly bribe, fight, or detour of 3+ hexes.
8. **Catastrophic Salvage Claim** — Another “independent” locks weapons (Ghost Ship vibes); hard contested check or lose the ability to complete a mission this round.
9. **Life Support Triage** — Choose: dump cargo/tokens, accept a lasting -1 to all rolls until repaired at a station, or skip two turns in cryo.
10. **Hunter Fleet** — A pursuit marker is placed on you; until cleared by a hard check or reaching a 0-tile sanctuary, every opponent interrupt against you succeeds automatically once per turn.
