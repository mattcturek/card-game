# Ship Deck (10) — LOCKED draft

Special abilities balanced roughly equal power.

| # | Name | Ability |
|---|------|---------|
| 1 | **Sparrow** | Once/turn: Move costs no phase if you move only 1 hex. |
| 2 | **Ironclad** | Attacks against you deal −2 damage (min 1). |
| 3 | **Specter** | Once/game: Hide without spending a phase. |
| 4 | **Sunlance** | Once/turn: +2 damage on one Attack. |
| 5 | **Courier** | Start: draw +1 main-deck card (6 opening). |
| 6 | **Bastion** | Once/game: prevent up to 5 damage. |
| 7 | **Vagrant** | After Move: peek one adjacent tile number. |
| 8 | **Quarry** | +1 AP regen (every 1 die roll instead of 2) while HP ≤ 10. |
| 9 | **Nomad** | Once/turn: Scan your tile only for free (no phase). |
| 10 | **Requiem** | When you would lose: instead set HP to 1 once/game. |
