# Card Game — Core Rules

Last updated: 2026-11-26 (America/Chicago)

Status: drafting with Matt. Locked items first; open questions at the bottom.

## Win condition

- Each player draws an **objective card** at random. That card is their primary mission.
- Missions are **secret**. No other player may see your objective.
- Other players may still try to **block or hinder** you from completing yours while pursuing their own.
- The **first player to complete their objective wins**.

## Board & movement

- Shared **hexagonal tile board**.
- **Background:** Milky Way as seen from Earth (see [board](board.md)).
- Each player chooses a **ship token** and starts at the **center** of the board.
- On your turn, **roll a die** to move your ship across hex tiles.
- Each tile has an **image** that shows what happens when you land on it (effects TBD by tile type).
- **Mission locations** are drawn on the board. Each mission card points a player to a specific location they must maneuver to. Completing the mission means resolving that destination as the card requires.
- **Feature sizes:** stars span a **2–6 tile radius**; planets span **1–4 tiles**.
- Every hex tile shows a number **0–3**.
- There are **three separate Event decks**: **Tier 1 Event**, **Tier 2 Event**, **Tier 3 Event**.
- Land on **0** → draw nothing. Land on **1 / 2 / 3** → draw a **Tier N Event** card.





## Content build order

1. Write the **mission card** set ([missions](missions.md)).
2. Place those mission-location tiles on the board.
3. Create the rest of the board (non-mission tiles).

## Turns & actions

- Play proceeds in **alternating turns**.
- Each turn you may take **2 actions**.
- Actions: **Move**, **Scan**, **Attack**.
- **Interrupts** can be played **at any time**. They are mixed into the main Event decks; effects are on the card. Cards TBD later.


## Move (locked)

- Costs 1 action. Roll a **d6**:
  - **Even** → move **2** spaces
  - **Odd** → move **1** space
- You may **skip** movement.
- After landing: tile **0** = no Event; **1/2/3** = draw that Tier Event.

## Scan (locked)

- Costs 1 action.
- Scans **your current tile and all adjacent tiles**.
- You **must Scan** before you can **Attack** (target must be on a scanned tile).

## Attack (locked)

- Costs 1 action. If **AP = 0**, you cannot Attack.
- Deal damage equal to your **current AP**, then spend **1 AP**.
- You may attack any ship/player on **your tile** or an **adjacent** tile (after Scan).


## HP & AP (locked)

- Start: **20 HP**, **20 AP**.
- **HP** never regenerates. **HP ≤ 0** → ship destroyed → **lose**.
- **AP** regenerates **+1 every 2 dice rolls**.

- Attacking subtracts **1 AP**.
