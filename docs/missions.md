## How missions work (locked)

- Each player draws one secret mission card.
- The card points the player to a **specific location** drawn on the board.
- First player to complete their mission wins.
- Completing a mission: TBD (reach location + resolve complication / checks).

## Card list (v1 — locked)

### M1 — Silent Relay at Station 9
- **Tone:** Friendly / Mystery
- **Board location:** Station 9 (unpowered deep-space communications relay)
- **Brief:** A deep-space communications relay on the edge of the sector went dark 48 hours ago, cutting off three mining colonies. Dock with the unpowered station, learn why the automated distress beacon was manually disabled from the inside, and restore the signal before a local raider fleet realizes the colonies are blind.
- **Complication:** The station's backup power only runs life support or the docking clamps—not both at once.

### M2 — The Cold Cargo Run
- **Tone:** Neutral / Smuggling
- **Board locations (2 tiles):** Colony Clinic Pickup + Mining Colony Dropoff (card tells you where each is)
- **Brief:** A desperate colony doctor hires your ship to haul six sealed cryo-containers of experimental vaccine past a corporate blockade. Spoof your transponder, bribe a dockmaster, or thread an uncharted asteroid belt to deliver before the containers thaw.
- **Complication:** One container's bio-signature isn't vaccine—it's a stowaway witness the corporation wants silenced.
- **Tiles:** locked — see [board](board.md)


### M3 — Ghost Ship on the Drift
- **Tone:** Friendly / Salvage & Rescue
- **Board locations:** Derelict survey ship (1 tile) + decaying gas giant (multi-tile cluster nearby)
- **Brief:** Long-range sensors pick up a century-old survey vessel still broadcasting a faint crew manifest. Navigate failing gravity plates and rogue maintenance drones to pull its navigation core (and anyone still in cryo-sleep) before the hull crushes in the upper atmosphere.
- **Complication:** Another independent ship just dropped out of warp claiming salvage rights and locking weapons.
- **Tiles:** locked — see [board](board.md)


### M4 — Blackout at Helios Refinery
- **Tone:** Hostile / Sabotage
- **Board location:** Helios Refinery — **one spaceship tile** (reach to pursue mission; more content later)
- **Brief:** A rival syndicate pays handsomely to knock out a competing fuel refinery's orbital stabilizer—just long enough to delay their shipment by a week, no casualties. Slip a crew member past security, plant a diagnostic virus in the reactor control room, and undock before lockdown.
- **Complication:** The refinery's chief engineer is an old acquaintance of your captain and recognizes your ship's registry.
- **Tiles:** locked — see [board](board.md)


### M5 — The Parley on Red Dust
- **Tone:** Friendly / Diplomacy
- **Board location:** Red Dust — **planet** (1–4 tiles); ship as neutral ground at/near that location
- **Brief:** Settlers and a nomadic salvage clan are hours from a shooting war over a newly discovered water-ice moon. Host both leaders, uncover who fired the first shot at a border buoy, and broker a truce before their gunboats arrive.
- **Complication:** Someone planted a listening bug in your briefing room; both delegations suspect you of working for the other side.
- **Tiles:** locked — see [board](board.md)
